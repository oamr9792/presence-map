Run things

One job runs at a time. Keys:

1. Get US subjects (Florida)

Downloads a sample of the state's free daily filing files (about 1 MB each) spread across the last few years, plus the most recent weeks, and imports a random sample of the officers named in them. Older appointments show the finished picture; recent ones are for measuring lag.

2. Get UK subjects (Companies House)

Needs COMPANIES_HOUSE_API_KEY. Imports directors of recently incorporated companies.

3. Check presence

Needs DataForSEO. Checks subjects not checked in the last 30 days against all target sites. Full mode is about 35 SERP calls per subject; cheap mode is 2. Runs several subjects at once; the scheduled job on Render also checks 100 subjects in cheap mode every Monday at 03:00 UTC.

4. Analyse

Rebuilds the trigger table, lags and rank stats from everything checked so far. Run after checks.
